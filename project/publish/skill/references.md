# Post copy: references

Companion to `SKILL.md` and `rules.json` (same ids). Compiled 2026-11-08, social performance data added 2026-11-09.

## Method

- Official pages and studies were opened and read on or before 2026-11-08 unless the note says 'seen in results' (only the search snippet was read, so the claim is second hand) or 'snippet' (the page is JS-rendered and was read through search snippets).
- Kinds: **official** (platform help, policy, API doc, engineering blog or a named platform lead), **study** (academic), **vendor** (a tool company's own data, usually observational and correlational), **creator** (a creator or industry newsletter), **observed** (our read of real posts, no performance claim).
- Labels used in `SKILL.md`: **measured** means a number from a study or official doc, with its sample. **Official** means a platform statement without numbers. **Observed** means a pattern in the examples below.
- Vendor studies measure correlation, not cause. Engagement-rate definitions differ between vendors and platforms, so never compare their percentages across studies.
- Observed examples came from Kino YouTube research (search ranking on 2026-11-08) for three tutorial queries. Ranking is not performance; no view counts were collected for this skill.

## Sources

### Studies

- **`vidiq-title-formulas-2026`**: [YouTube Title Formulas Study (vidIQ Research)](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-title-formulas-study/) (2026-07-15). Vendor. 4M+ 2026 uploads, relative views where 1.0x is average. Question-ending titles 0.82x; plain how-to with no twist 1.07x; 'Turning X into Y' over 3x; 'do not / stop / never' near 2x; 'X days/hours' near 1.5x; specific superlative over 1.5x. Specific story frames beat generic formulas. No length data, no niche split.
- **`air-titles-2026`**: [How to write a YouTube title that gets clicked: research across 11 niches (AIR Media-Tech)](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/how-to-write-a-youtube-title-that-gets-clicked-research-across-11-niches) (updated 2026-06-12). Vendor. 18,080 channels, metric is engagement rate (likes plus comments over views), correlational. 30 to 50 chars best overall, under 30 best in Gaming, 90+ worst. Gaming: one ALL CAPS word positive, questions weak, brackets least effective. Search content: keyword in the first 40% of the title. Browse content: lead with the hook.
- **`overseeros-howto-2026`**: [Do 'How to' YouTube titles get more views? (OverseerOS)](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/do-how-to-youtube-titles-get-more-views) (2026-09-03). Vendor. 1,794 videos from 49 channels. 'How to' titles: median 1.60x channel baseline vs 0.99x; 41.4% reached 2x+ vs 27.7%. Authors credit search intent and topic, not the phrase. How-to titles median 55 chars, 10 words.
- **`socialinsider-captions-2026`**: [Social media caption length best practices (Socialinsider)](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-caption-length-best-practices/) (2026-10-27). Vendor. Organic posts Jan to Sep 2026: 9.66M TikTok, 28.6M Instagram, 38.5M Facebook, 897K LinkedIn. TikTok under 30 words 4.90% vs 90+ words 3.90%. Reels 30 to 90 words 0.25% vs 0.20%. Facebook Reels 30 to 60 words 0.28% vs 0.15% under 30; link posts 0.05% or less. LinkedIn video 4.2 to 4.5% at any length; text posts under 30 words 2.40% vs about 3.7%.
- **`buffer-x-links-2025`**: [Links on X: what the data says (Buffer)](https://buffer.com/resources/links-on-x/) (2025-10-14). Vendor. 18.8M posts, 71K accounts. Non-Premium link posts at 0% median engagement since March 2025; text about 0.40%, video about 0.25%. Premium link posts about 0.28%, text about 0.90%. Link in a reply 'can sometimes avoid the distribution penalty'.
- **`buffer-engagement-2026`**: [State of Social Media Engagement 2026 (Buffer)](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/) (2026-03-05). Vendor. 52M+ posts. Replying to comments lifts engagement: LinkedIn +30%, Threads +42%, Instagram +21%, Facebook +9.5%, X +8%, Bluesky +5%. Link posts trail video and text on LinkedIn, Threads, X and Bluesky.
- **`metricool-hashtags-2026`**: [How many hashtags to use on each network (Metricool)](https://metricool.com/how-many-hashtags-to-use/) (2026-06-29). Vendor. Instagram study of 24M+ posts from 375K accounts: posts with hashtags got 32% fewer views and 34% fewer interactions (correlational). Suggests IG 3 to 5, TikTok 3 to 5, YouTube 2 to 3, Facebook 0 to 3, X max 2.
- **`ordinal-li-links`**: [LinkedIn link penalty study (Ordinal)](https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/linkedin-link-penalty-study) (undated, read 2026-11). Vendor. 900K+ posts: link posts got 26.5% less reach. Contested: Metricool reported +13.57% interactions for LinkedIn link posts (577K posts, seen in results). Ordinal's [hashtag study](https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/linkedin-hashtags) (270K posts) found no-hashtag posts averaged more impressions.
- **`eden-507k-2026`**: [What makes a good YouTube thumbnail? We analyzed 507,478 videos (Eden)](https://eden.so/blog/what-makes-a-good-youtube-thumbnail/) (2026). Vendor. Thumbnail text repeating the title verbatim: 6.3% of breakouts vs 6.8% of underperformers, so the pairing rarely repeats words. Observational.
- **`etra-2018-eyetracking`**: [YouTube thumbnail and title attention, eye tracking (ETRA 2018)](https://zenodo.org/records/3754785/files/2018_ETRA_youtube_attention-5pages-websitepreprint.pdf) (2018). Study. Lab eye tracking while browsing: viewers read thumbnail and title together. Small sample, older.

### Official docs

- **`yt-hashtags-help`**: [Use hashtags for YouTube videos (YouTube Help)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658) (undated, read 2026-11). Official. Up to 3 description hashtags show above the title. Over 60 hashtags and YouTube ignores all of them. Over-tagging can remove a video from search.
- **`yt-tags-help`**: [Add tags to your YouTube videos (YouTube Help)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402) (undated, read 2026-11). Official. Tags 'play a minimal role' in discovery; useful for common misspellings. Title, thumbnail and description matter more.
- **`yt-edit-settings-help`**: [Edit video settings (YouTube Help)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404) (undated, read 2026-11). Official. Title max 100 chars, description max 5,000. Manual chapters override automatic ones.
- **`yt-description-tips`**: [Tips for video descriptions (YouTube Help)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449) (undated, read 2026-11). Official. The first lines are what viewers see first. Pick 1 to 2 main words and feature them in both title and description. Unique description per video, use chapters.
- **`yt-chapters-help`**: [Video chapters (YouTube Help)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579) (undated, read 2026-11). Official. First timestamp 00:00, at least 3 in ascending order, each chapter at least 10 seconds.

## Patterns across the examples (observed)

- Server tutorials that rank put the game name and 'server' in the first 5 words. Curiosity titles without the game name are the exception.
- Freshness tags (year, version, month) are everywhere because mods and games update often. Use one only when it is true and on screen.
- Time and 'easy' claims are common and checkable against the runtime. Ours must be too.
- Emoji appear in a few titles (fire, star). They add nothing to search and break the house voice.

## Not verified

- Instagram's cap of 5 hashtags: announced via @creators and reported widely, but the Help Center could not be read and the Graph API doc still says 30. Some vendors still say 30.
- TikTok's cap of 5 hashtags (vendor and press only) and vendor claims of a 4,000-character TikTok caption. We use 2,200 per `format.md`.
- YouTube title truncation (vendors say about 70 chars desktop search, 50 to 60 mobile). No official number.
- Feed folds: Instagram about 125 chars, LinkedIn about 140 mobile and 210 desktop. Vendor observations.
- Facebook Reels title limit of 255 (`ayrshare-char-limits`). Current status of the Facebook link-limit test.
- LinkedIn link penalty: studies disagree; LinkedIn has said nothing official.
- The X Help Center advice of max 2 hashtags was reported but not read. Whether '#shorts' still does anything on YouTube.
- Clickable links: TikTok and Instagram captions do not make URLs clickable. This is common knowledge from use, not a cited doc.
